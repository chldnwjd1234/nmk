Make the World

The National Museum of Korea connects Korean culture
with the world, embracing global history and art.
Rooted in diversity and inclusion, it strives to create a
museum open to all. Through AI and digital innovation,
it redefines the way we experience heritage.
As Korea’s leading cultural brand,
it continues to grow as a museum for the world.

Architecture and Design

The National Museum of Korea blends traditional Korean architecture with modern design, emphasizing harmony, simplicity, and minimal ornamentation. Set between Namsan Mountain and Mirror Pond, it reflects the balance of yin and yang. The open plaza, inspired by maru, connects exhibition and performance spaces while integrating with nature and gardens. Located at the southern gateway of Yongsan Park, the museum serves as a central cultural landmark in Seoul.
