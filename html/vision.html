Create Life

Enhanced accessibility for all ages and backgrounds, Barrier-free routes and easy-to-understand guides, Continuou community programs and participatory activities, Open data and images to promote reuse.

Lead the Future

Advanced digital collections (3D, IIIF) and online exhibitions AI and data-driven research and conservation Standardized interactive and immersive displays Sustainable, eco-friendly operations.

Make the World

The National Museum of Korea connects Korean culture with the world, embracing global history and art. Rooted in diversity and inclusion, it strives to create a museum open to all. Through AI and digital innovation, it redefines the way we experience heritage.As Korea’s leading cultural brand, it continues to grow as a museum for the world.
