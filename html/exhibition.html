Collectors of Light

From Impressionism to early Modernism, artists redefined vision itself through the study of light.

The Collectors of Light presents 81 works from The Met’s Robert Lehman Collection, celebrating both the painters who captured light and the collector who devoted his life to preserving it.

Once seen as radical, Impressionism became the language that opened the path to modern art — inviting us to reflect on how the act of seeing transforms creation.
