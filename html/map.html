Museum Map

The Medieval and Early Modern History section highlights Korea's development through the Goryeo, Joseon, and Korean Empire periods, showcasing key events, conflicts, and cultural achievements.

The Prehistory and Ancient History section displays artifacts that represent some of the earliest evidence of Korean civilization and culture.

Calligraphy and Painting Gallery highlights the elegance of Korean brush art and Buddhist paintings. Wood and Lacquer Crafts Gallery showcases the refined beauty of Korean craftsmanship.

The Donated Collection Gallery is a space that showcases cultural heritage items gifted to the National Museum of Korea. It honors their donors and inspires reflection on the significance and meaning of philanthropy.

The Room of Quiet Contemplation features two pensive bodhisattva statues, each designated as National Treasure of Korea.

The Sculpture and Crafts section displays many of the world’s finest examples of Buddhist sculpture, metal arts, and ceramics, demonstrating the traditions and astonishing expertise of Korean artisans.

The south exhibition space is dedicated to the presentation of Asian art and culture. It consists of six galleries, each of which deals with India-Southeast Asia, Central Asia, China, Japan, and ancient Greece and Rome.
