Visit Reservation

Collectors of Light

Directions

The reservation will be automatically canceled if you do not enter the museum 30 minutes after the session starts.

(*Cancelled tickets will become available for individual online reservation.)

[No-show Policy]

If you have not entered by 30 minutes after the start of the session without cancellation, visiting of the children's museum will be restricted for 60 days from the date of a no-show, and all reservations within the restricted period will be cancelled.

Reservation Guide

  1. Visitors are required to make reservations online (telephone reservations are not accepted).
  2. Reservation for up to five people can be made at once, and reservations can only be made once per day (including individual and group).
  3. Online reservations are available from 00:00 14 days before the date of visit to 50 minutes after the start of each session.

Admission Guide

  1. On-site ticketing is not available. Reserved mobile tickets must be checked at the entrance to the museum.
  2. Admission of visitors without children or children without adult guardians is restricted.
  3. Reservations need to be made for toddlers under 36 months old.

Additional Information

  1. Private docent tours (e.g. field trips) and lectures are prohibited.
  2. If you wish to visit the museum for a field survey, you must receive permission in advance over the phone and in written form. Refer to the Children's Museum website.
  3. For further information, Contact +82-2-2077-9647, 9648.
