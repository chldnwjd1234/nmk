By car

  • Toward City Hall

    Toward Itaewon → Before entering Banpogyo (Bridge), make a right turn toward Yongsan Family Park
    → Go straight approximately 300 meters from the entrance of Yongsan Family Park → The National Museum of Korea is on the right.

  • Toward Gangnam

    Banpodaegyo Bridge → At the first 3-way intersection, make a left turn toward Yongsan Family Park 
    → Go straight approximately 300 meters from the entrance of Yongsan Family Park → The National Museum of Korea is on the right.

  • Toward Samgakji

    Make a right turn toward Yongsan Station (Subway Line 1)→
    Make a left turn toward Yongsan Family Park and go strait approximately 150 meters from Ichon Station. → The National Museum of Korea is on the left..

※ Parking spaces are limited. It’s recommended to visit by bus or subway. ※ Parking fee is calculated by an electronic parking meter system. You can exit the parking lot quicker by using the unmanned meter of the underground parking lot.
